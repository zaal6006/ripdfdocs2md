# **I. Purpose** 

The objective of this policy is to define and allow for reimbursement of personally incurred research expenses on a fair basis, providing the greatest possible flexibility. The policy relies on the goodwill and discretion of the employee, student and supervisor, while at the same time allowing the RI-MUHC to meet its stewardship obligations. 

# **II. Persons/Areas Affected:** 

Researchers, employees, volunteers, consultants, students, guest speakers/presenters, visiting researchers, contract employees, and management. 

The policy applies to reimbursement of expenses from all sources of funding administered by the RI-MUHC. When funding agency guidelines, or the terms of a specific award, impose greater restrictions, the more stringent requirements will apply. 

# **III. Definition of Terms:** 

Per Diems: Allowance to cover non-local meal expenses incurred whilst travelling Claimant: Individual seeking reimbursement of personal expenses incurred 

# **IV. Policy:** 

The RI-MUHC will reimburse expenses that are legitimate, reasonable and appropriate for the research-related activity undertaken, when and/if it meets the terms and conditions imposed by the funding source and when it has been personally incurred by the claimant. 

# **IV-1** <u>Non-eligible Personal Expenses</u> 

Expenses deemed personal in nature are ineligible for reimbursement and non-refundable. Examples of personal expenses are: 

- Passport application or renewal fees including photos; 

- Travel, hotel and/or meal costs for family members or travel guests; 

- Credit card membership, administration and interest fees; 

- Airline or vendor loyalty membership fees; 

# **IV-2** 

# <u>Reimbursement Claim Time Limitations</u> 

Reimbursement claim(s) should be submitted within 30 days of the trip/event/purchase or latest dated receipt. Claims that refer to prior fiscal years, or older than 1 year will be refused. 

# **IV-3** <u>Travel/Event Advances</u> 

Travel /Event Advances may be requested to cover upfront expenses. Unspent advances must be returned within 30 days of the end of the trip or event via a check or e-transfer. A claimant may hold a maximum of three open advances at one time. 

# **IV-4** <u>Currency of Claim</u> 

Reimbursement claims are payable in Canadian or US Dollars, and only one currency per expense claim. Supporting invoices and receipts in a currency different to that of the claim must be converted to the currency of the claim and both the exchange rate used for the conversion and the source of the exchange rate used clearly identified on or attached to the receipt document. A snapshot of the daily rate following the webpage of Bank of Canada is acceptable as a source <u>https://www.bankofcanada.ca/rates/exchange/daily-exchange-rates-lookup/</u> and will be applied in absence of a proof. 

# **IV-5** <u>Method of Payment</u> 

The RI-MUHC method of reimbursement for all Canadian dollar claims is by direct bank deposit. US Dollar claim reimbursement will be issued by cheque. A Direct deposit authorization form and a void check or bank letter  is required for Canadian payees. 

# **IV-6** <u>Conference/Convention Agenda</u> 

A complete conference prospectus/agenda must be attached to your claim. In the case that a prospectus/agenda is not available, alternative supporting documentation showing the purpose, dates and location of the event such as an e-mail from the event organizer can be submitted. 

# **IV-7** <u>One Claim per Trip/Event</u> 

Each claimant for each trip/event must make a separate reimbursement claim. The purpose of the trip/event must be described in sufficient detail to allow for approval. 

# **IV-8** <u>Receipts, Proof of Payment and Credit Card Statements</u> 

Original, scanned or copied documents are accepted but must be of good quality and clearly legible. 

Receipts must clearly show the vendor name, description of what is purchased, the amounts charged and Canadian sales tax registration numbers where applicable and must be attached to the reimbursement claim. Receipts should clearly separate expenses incurred by the claimant from those of other expenses (such as travel or meal guests) that are not refundable. 

Proof of payment for all non-cash items is required and must be attached to the reimbursement claim. Examples of proof of payment are credit card receipts, payment confirmation receipts or emails, zero-balance invoice statements. 

Foreign exchange receipts without supporting information of the rate will be converted to Canadian Dollars at the rate in force on the date of the transaction as per the Bank of Canada Exchange Rate tables (please refer to IV-4 currency of claim). 

# **IV-9** <u>Lost Receipts</u> 

Where the claimant certifies that a receipt was lost (except for meals, please refer to meals section for details) accidentally destroyed or unattainable, a personal declaration may replace the receipt if made in good faith. 

# **IV-10** <u>Levels of Approval</u> 

All expenses submitted for reimbursement must be approved according to the account privileges attached to the cost center being charged. Travel and Meals reimbursement claims made by researchers or visiting researchers must be approved by the researcher’s Program Leader or 

Department/Division head and those made by students by the research grant holder. A claimant cannot self-approve a travel or meal expense reimbursement claim. 

# **IV-11** <u>Affiliation of Claimant to Research Project</u> 

The affiliation to the Research Project refunding the expense must be clearly identified on the Payment Requisition Claim. 

# **IV-12** <u>Accommodation</u> 

The standard for accommodation is a reasonably priced single room in a safe environment conveniently located and comfortably equipped. If staying with friends or family, the daily fee that may be claimed is $25.00 CAN _._ The name and location of the family member or friend must be provided with the expense claim. 

# **IV-13** <u>Transportation</u> 

Claimants should make every effort to select the most cost efficient medium of transport and the RI-MUHC reserves the right to refuse claims when this is not the case. 

# Vehicle Rental: 

- The standard for vehicle rentals is mid-size.  Vehicles rented beyond the mid-size range may be authorized based on factors such as, but not limited to, safety, the needs of the claimant, and the bulk or weight of goods transported; 

- Gasoline is an eligible expense. Ensure that the gas tank is filled before returning the car to avoid a costly gasoline surcharge; 

- The claimant is responsible for securing adequate insurance coverage and reimbursement is conditional upon granting agency or sponsor eligibility guidelines; 

# Personal Vehicle 

- The mileage reimbursement rate for the use of a personal vehicle is $0.60/kilometer plus parking charges, if applicable. Proof of mileage from a reliable source such as Google Maps, is required showing the point of departure to destination. 

- No gasoline claims are accepted when claiming per km allowance. 

- Kilometer allowance claims should not exceed the actual cost of equivalent rail or economy air fare; 

# Rail 

- The standard for rail travel is the least expensive economy class fare 

# Airfare 

- The standard class of service is the economy class fare. Seats in a fare class greater than economy will be considered only for flights with duration more than 5 hours or due to medical reasons ( supervisor ‘s approval required); 

- The E-ticket or air-fare invoice with destination and charges details must be included with the Expense Claim (boarding passes are not required nor will be used as a substitute for an e-ticket) ; 

- Flight Cancellation Insurance, baggage and seat reservation fees are deemed to be eligible expenses; 

- No reimbursements can be claimed for air tickets purchased with personal loyalty program points;

# **IV-14** <u>Meal Reimbursements</u> 

# _Non-local Meal Expenses_ 

Claims for meal expenses incurred outside the Greater Montreal area (or home city for claimants not based in the Greater Montreal area) while at conferences or during business trips will be reimbursed at actual cost if supported by a detailed itemized receipt, as well as proof of payment. The full name of all participants must be listed, as well as the purpose of the event. Lost receipts declarations are not accepted. Where no itemized detailed receipt(s) is available, a reprint copy receipt is required at the local restaurant. 

The per diem allowance is not applicable where meals are included in transportation charges, conference registration fees or hotel rates/receipts unless allergy and/or religious prohibition is declared. 

The per diem allowance for meals is: 

| Canada | $80.00 per day |
|---|---|
| United States | $100.00 per day |
| Other | $100.00 per day |

All per diem rates are in Canadian dollars and exclude reasonable amount of gratuities. 

# _Local Hospitality_ 

Local hospitality defined as events held for entertaining employees and nonemployees (e.g. visiting speakers) for special events and/or other singular activities (teambuilding activities, celebrations, recruitment, donor relations, fund raising, training, business meetings etc.). 

Claimants must list the full names of all participants as well as the purpose of the event and attach supporting receipts and proof of payment. Per diems cannot be claimed for local hospitality events. Refundable eligible events and maximum refundable amounts are identified in Appendix I: Reimbursements for Local Hospitality and Entertainment. 

# _Alcohol_ 

The maximum allowable claim is $25 CAN per person per event subject to funding agency eligibility. When funding agency guidelines, or the terms of a specific award, impose greater restrictions (e.g. CIHR, NIH), the more stringent requirements will apply. 

# **V Procedures:** 

Reimbursement claims for personally paid expenses must be submitted using a RI-MUHC Payment Requisition web Form accessed via the RI-MUHC Portal.  Supporting documentation and proof of payment must be attached to the claim. Incomplete expense claims will result in delayed reimbursements and may be returned.
