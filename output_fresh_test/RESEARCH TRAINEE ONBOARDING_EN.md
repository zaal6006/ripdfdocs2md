# **Overview** 

Welcome to the **Research Institute of the McGill University Health Centre (The Institute)** . This **Onboarding Guide** is designed to help you navigate the key resources, training, and supports available to you as a research trainee at The Institute. 

As part of both the McGill University and MUHC communities, The Institute offers a unique environment where trainees learn and work alongside world-class scientists and clinicians. We are here to help you develop the skills, knowledge, and connections needed to make the most of your research experience. 

## **Note** 

This guide is intended to complement the **Research Trainee Handbook** , which you should review before starting. Consult with your **supervisor (PI)** and **Program Assistant** or **Coordinator** or **CORE Assistant** for guidance specific to your program and research environment. 

The onboarding process is organized into the following sequential phases: 

- Before you begin 

- Upon start date (First day and first few weeks) 

- During your traineeship 

Complete each step in a timely manner to ensure a smooth start to your traineeship. For additional assistance, explore <u>the Portal</u> for helpful contacts and further information.
